Handwriting Eraser — Installation

1. Drag Handwriting Eraser.app into Applications.
2. Launch Handwriting Eraser from Applications.
3. On first use, choose Download Local AI Model (about 176 MB).

macOS 15 or later is required. The universal app supports Apple silicon and Intel.

Some releases are not notarized by Apple. If macOS blocks the app, go to
System Settings > Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway for Handwriting
Eraser. Only do this if you trust the source and have checked the release's
SHA-256 checksum. A signed and notarized release does not need this step.

PDF pages stay on your Mac. Always review output: handwriting can remain and
printed content can be erased. Exported PDFs are rasterized.
