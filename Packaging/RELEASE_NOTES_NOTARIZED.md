## Installation

1. Download the universal macOS DMG and, if desired, verify its accompanying SHA-256 checksum.
2. Open the DMG and drag **Handwriting Eraser.app** into Applications.
3. On first use, install the local handwriting detector (about 176 MB).

This build is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. It requires macOS 15 or later and supports Apple silicon and Intel. PDF pages stay on your Mac.

Review output carefully: handwriting may remain or printed content may be removed. PDFs are exported as rasterized pages; this is not a redaction tool.
